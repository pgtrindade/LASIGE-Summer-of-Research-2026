Research Excellence & Innovation

Join LASIGE Summer of Research: Shaping Tomorrow's Researchers

LASIGE is a premier research center at the Faculdade de Ciências da Universidade de Lisboa, recognized with the highest excellence rating from Portugal's Foundation for Science and Technology. Here, we're not just conducting research — we're shaping the digital future through groundbreaking work.

Within our community of 250+ researchers at the University of Lisbon, you'll find yourself contributing to seven Research Lines of Excellence that address real-world challenges, from cyber-physical systems and healthcare informatics to secure decentralized systems and inclusive human-computer interaction. LASIGE advantage extends beyond our labs. Our researchers regularly publish in top-tier journals, secure competitive European funding, and collaborate with industry partners that transform theoretical advances into tangible solutions. Many of our students have presented at international leading conferences, building professional networks that have led to positions at international organizations, such as Google, Intel, Meta, and Microsoft, and top universities worldwide.

Don't just study the future of computing — help create it at LASIGE, where we're not just pursuing excellence in computer science and engineering — we're redefining it.

Hands-On Research Experience

Our 3-month summer program (July-September) offers undergraduate and first-year master students direct involvement in cutting-edge research projects, with mentorship from LASIGE researchers.

Comprehensive Training

Gain valuable skills through workshops covering research fundamentals (literature reviews, academic writing) and technical training (machine learning, data analysis).

Supportive Community

Become part of a vibrant research environment with academic and social events, building connections with fellow students and experienced researchers while discovering your research potential.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who can apply to the Summer of Research?

The program is open to undergraduate and first year master's students interested in research and innovation in Computer Science and related areas.

Should the CV and motivation letter be written in English?

Yes. As a general rule, all application documents and responses should be written in English, especially for projects supervised by international mentors, since they will be responsible for evaluating the applications.

I will finish my degree this year. Can I still apply to the Summer of Research?

Yes. Students who are completing their degree this academic year are still eligible to apply, as long as they meet the program requirements.

Can I apply to more than one project?

Yes. Applicants may apply to multiple projects (up to 5). All selected projects must be ranked in order of preference, as this ranking will be taken into account during the selection process. You should also ensure that your motivation letter is relevant to the selected projects.

Are international students allowed to apply?

Yes. International students are welcome to apply, and some projects may be conducted entirely in English.

How are candidates selected?

Candidates are evaluated based on their academic background, CV, motivation letter, relevant skills, and, where applicable, their answers to project-specific questions. In some cases, candidates may also be invited to an interview with the mentor.

Can I participate if I have no previous research experience?

Yes. Previous research experience is not mandatory. Motivation, curiosity, and willingness to learn are highly valued.

Will participants receive a scholarship or financial support?

Some projects may provide a research grant or other financial support. This will be specified within Individual project descriptions. For more information, please contact the organization or assigned mentors post candidate selection.

If I am applying to more than one project, can I be selected for multiple?

Applicants may apply to multiple projects, but selected participants will normally be assigned to only one project. In exceptional cases (e.g., if selected for more than one project by the same mentor) the applicant may be asked to select between assigned projects.

Is participation remote, in person, or hybrid?

The working format may vary by project, although lab stays and in-person meetings with mentors are encouraged whenever possible. Some curricular activities will be mandatory and take place in person. The program will include a break of a few weeks in August to accommodate summer holidays. During this period, participants and mentors may remain in contact remotely, by mutual agreement.
